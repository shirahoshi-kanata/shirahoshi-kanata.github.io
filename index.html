TikTok Content Posting API

Publish and manage TikTok content
— built for creators

KANATA SNS POST is a cross-posting and content management service that lets creators connect their TikTok accounts and publish videos safely, with full control over privacy settings, interactions, and branded-content disclosure.

Key Features

🔗TikTok Account Linking

Secure OAuth connection with transparent account selection. You always know which account you are posting to.

🛡️Privacy-First Publishing

No default privacy levels. Every post requires an explicit choice — Public, Friends, or Private — with independent comment, Duet, and Stitch controls.

📤Cross-Platform Workflow

Prepare your content once and manage distribution across platforms from a single, clear interface.

KANATA SNS POST について

KANATA SNS POST は、クリエイターの皆様がTikTokアカウントを安全に連携し、 動画の投稿・管理を行うためのコンテンツ管理サービスです。プライバシー設定や コメント・Duet・Stitchなどの公開範囲を、投稿のたびに明示的に選択できます。
