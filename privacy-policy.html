1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how KANATA SNS POST (operated by Shirahoshi Kanata, "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and handles information in connection with our content management service for TikTok creators, including integration with TikTok's platform via the official TikTok Content Posting API.

2. Information We Collect

When you use the Service, we may collect the following types of information:

  • TikTok account information you choose to connect (public profile data, account identifier) obtained through the TikTok API with your explicit consent
  • Content you publish or prepare through the Service (video files, captions, privacy settings)
  • Usage data necessary for the operation and improvement of the Service
  • Contact information you voluntarily provide when reaching out to us

We do not collect sensitive personal information such as passwords, financial data, or government identification.

3. How We Use the Information

Information collected is used exclusively for:

  • Providing the content publishing and management features you request
  • Facilitating cross-posting workflows through TikTok's Content Posting API
  • Improving the Service and responding to support inquiries

We do not sell, rent, or share personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. TikTok API Usage

Our use of the TikTok API complies with TikTok's API Developer Policy and TikTok's Privacy Policy. Data obtained through the TikTok API is used only for the purposes described in this policy, with your consent, and is not stored beyond what is necessary for operational functions.

5. Data Retention

Usage and publishing data is retained only as long as necessary for the operation of the Service. Data obtained via the TikTok API is not stored permanently. You may request deletion of any data associated with you by contacting us.

6. Third-Party Services

The Service integrates with the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policies:

  • TikTok – account connection, content publishing, and management
  • GitHub Pages – hosting of this website

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to data we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your data
  • Disconnect your TikTok account from the Service at any time
  • Opt out of any data collection at any time

To exercise these rights, please contact us at the address below.

8. Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the information we process. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
